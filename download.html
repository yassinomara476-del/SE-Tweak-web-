FOUR STEPS

From purchase to play.

01

Choose a plan

1 month for EGP 300, 3 months for EGP 800, or lifetime use of the licensed current version for EGP 1,000.

02

Receive your files

The administrator verifies the purchase and sends the appropriate PRO installer privately. Ask separately for the Free Tools build.

03

Enter the License Key

Open PRO, enter the key and activate. The first successful activation binds the license to that device.

04

Check updates

Updates are a separate entitlement. If you purchase updates, use the optional update check in the app.

Before installing: only run a file received through a channel you trust; keep your key private; the administrator can reset a device binding when legitimate hardware changes require it.
