Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Website analytics — optional

The website does not record analytics visits until you explicitly choose “Allow analytics” in the consent banner. If you accept, the service may record the connection IP address supplied by the hosting/proxy network, an anonymous random browser token, the page path, a limited referrer URL, browser user-agent text and timestamp. IP information may be unavailable or proxy-derived. The anonymous token is not a verified identity and does not tell the owner your real name.

If you select Reject, the optional visit event is not sent. You can clear or change your choice by clearing this site's local storage. Necessary technical requests to load the site may still be handled by the hosting provider.

License and device data

When a customer activates the Windows app, the licensing service processes the license key, a one-way hash derived from the Windows machine identity, app version, activation/validation timestamps and connection IP information for one-device licensing, support, expiration checks and abuse prevention. The app stores its successful license state encrypted for the current Windows user. The product's free cleanup tools run locally.

Retention and use

Data is used for product operation, subscription administration, security and basic analytics. The administrator should remove records that are no longer needed and handle customer requests under applicable law. Do not submit passwords, payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive data through the website.

Third-party services

The website and license service are hosted using GitHub Pages and Supabase. If you open Discord or another third-party site, that provider's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Your choices and contact

Rejecting optional analytics does not block the website. To ask about license records or request correction/deletion, contact the administrator through the official Discord or sales channel configured on the website.

Before public launch, replace the placeholder Discord invite with your real official support link and add any business-specific contact and retention details required in your jurisdiction.
